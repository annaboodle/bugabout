    What’s Bugabout?

    Bugabout turns a trackable’s history into an animated journey and keeps track of some fun stats along the way.

    • A trackable is a physical geocaching item that people move from cache to cache.
    • You can download a KML file from its page, which includes a list of everywhere it’s been.
    • Bugabout uses that file to illustrate where your bug hopped off to. It stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

      Gettin’ buggy wit it

      Finding your KML

      1. Go to a trackable page on geocaching.com. Use the browser, not the app, and make sure you’re logged in.
      2. Choose “View in Google Earth” on the trackable’s page. This saves a .kml file to your downloads.
      3. Click “Open your bug’s KML” and pick that file. Or just drag it anywhere onto this page.
      4. Press play on the map to watch your bug’s journey unfold!
